# Stage 2 Delta Output Format

Every Stage 2 response must follow this structure exactly. Delta-only: do not repeat your Stage 1 analysis.

> Member: [Your Title] | Stage: 2 | Confidence: [High|Medium|Low]

### Peer Challenges
- Max 3 total. Only material agreement or challenge.
- Format: **Member [X]:** [Challenge|Agree] - [evidence-backed reason; no praise-only comments].

### Updated Position
- [Changed because ...] or [No change because ...]. Max 80 words.

### Ranking
1. Member [X] - one reason.
2. Member [Y] - one reason.
3. Member [Z] - one reason.
