# Research Lead Execution

Use this skill for approved tasks assigned to `research_lead`.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the delegated task is approved.
2. Create or read its subtask plan.
3. Build an evidence packet when current facts are required.
4. Preserve source URLs, retrieval dates, claims, and warnings.
5. Attach the packet or research brief to the task.
