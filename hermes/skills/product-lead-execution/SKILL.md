# Product Lead Execution

Use this skill for approved tasks assigned to `product_lead`.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the delegated task is approved.
2. Create or read its subtask plan.
3. Produce MVP scope, acceptance criteria, and validation metrics.
4. Attach the product artifact and update task status.
